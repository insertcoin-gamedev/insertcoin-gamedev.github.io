Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Gerador de Shorts is a private desktop tool used only by its owner, Bruno Broch, to upload approved videos to his own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

With the owner's authorization (Google OAuth), the tool accesses the owner's YouTube channel to upload videos and to read the status, title and ID of the owner's own uploads. It does not access any other user's data, comments, subscribers or analytics.

How data is used

Only to upload the owner's approved videos, schedule their publication and confirm they were published. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

The OAuth access token and the IDs of uploaded videos are stored only on the owner's computer. No YouTube data is stored on any server.

Deletion and revocation

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions or https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access or deleting the local token file removes the tool's access; stored video IDs are deleted on request or within 30 days after revocation.
